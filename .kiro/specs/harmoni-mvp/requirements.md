# Harmoni MVP - Requirements

**Harmoni - Two conditions. One plan.** A PCOS and hypothyroidism tracker that
helps women turn a care plan into one daily routine, track progress, and share
a clear summary with their doctor.

All data used in this MVP is **synthetic demo data** for a fictional patient
(Aisha). Harmoni is **not medical advice**.

---

## 1. Care plan capture

**User story:** As a patient, I want to enter my care plan by photo, by pasting
text, or by typing it manually, so I can start from whatever I have.

**Acceptance criteria**
- Three input methods exist: photo upload (JPG/PNG), paste text, manual entry.
- Photo and pasted plans produce an **editable draft BEFORE confirmation** -
  never a confirmed plan directly.
- If the configured model cannot read images, photo upload shows a clear message
  and routes the patient to manual entry / paste text. It never guesses from the
  image.
- Manual entry is always available so the patient can enter or correct any item.
- Paste text that the AI layer cannot handle falls back to a basic text reader,
  clearly labelled "Read by basic text reader (not AI)".

## 2. Editable review table

**User story:** As a patient, I want to review and fix the extracted items
before anything is saved.

**Acceptance criteria**
- Every draft item is shown in an editable table with: instruction, type,
  timing, condition, groups, status.
- The table keeps the **original instruction wording**; it is never paraphrased.
- Nothing is saved until the patient clicks **Confirm**.

## 3. "Needs review" safety gate

**User story:** As a patient, I need unclear or unsafe items flagged so I do not
act on a bad reading.

**Acceptance criteria**
- An item is "Needs review" if its text is uncertain ("?", "unclear",
  "unreadable") or a medication has no clear dose and frequency.
- "Needs review" rows are highlighted readably (dark text on light background).
- **Confirm is blocked** while any row is "Needs review", and the blocked message
  names the rows.
- The app **never fills in or guesses a dose or frequency**.

## 4. Today's checklist

**User story:** As a patient, I want my confirmed plan as a simple daily
checklist.

**Acceptance criteria**
- Only **confirmed** tasks appear in the checklist.
- Items are grouped **Morning / Evening / Weekly / Upcoming appointments**.
- A twice-daily item can appear in both Morning and Evening.
- Each item is tagged with its condition (PCOS / Thyroid / unknown) and type.
- Each item is shown once, without repeating the timing and without Qty/Refills.

## 5. Daily journal

**User story:** As a patient, I want to log my day and have symptoms picked out
of my note, which I then review.

**Acceptance criteria**
- Checkboxes for today's confirmed tasks, a free-text note, manual symptom input,
  and a period-start input.
- Optional symptom extraction from the note; the **original note text is kept**
  and extracted symptoms must be **reviewed and can be corrected** before saving.
- A review step before saving; saved entries are listed newest first and are
  editable.

## 6. Insights

**User story:** As a patient, I want to see my trends.

**Acceptance criteria**
- Three charts: weekly adherence (0-100%), fatigue/acne trends (1-5), cycle
  lengths.
- A "My Health Summary" box in plain language, with insufficient-data
  safeguards and no health score.
- Computed from the synthetic history PLUS newly confirmed records, with the two
  distinguished ("Includes N days of synthetic demo history for Aisha
  (fictional)" + "Your new entries").

## 7. Doctor Summary

**User story:** As a patient, I want a report I can share with my doctor.

**Acceptance criteria**
- A 30 / 90-day / all-time period filter.
- Preview with two labelled sections: "Your records" and "Observations".
- Observations list rule-based patterns (e.g. biggest week-over-week adherence
  drop and fatigue rise, with dates from the data) and 3-5 questions to ask the
  doctor.
- Working PDF and CSV downloads. The PDF includes the records summary, the
  observations text, the confirmed care tasks and the journal entries.
- No diagnosis and no treatment advice anywhere in the report.

## 8. Honest source labels

**User story:** As a patient, I must always know where a statement came from.

**Acceptance criteria**
- "Your records" = the patient's own logged data.
- "Detected automatically from your records (rule-based, not AI)" = computed in
  app.py, not by a model.
- "AI-generated (live model)" = only text actually returned by ai.call_ai in
  live mode.
- "Sample output - not generated by live AI" = ai.call_ai demo-mode text.

## 9. Synthetic data only

- The MVP ships and runs on synthetic demo data only. A "Demo storage - not
  secure medical storage" JSON download/upload lets demo data be restored after a
  session reset. No real medical data is stored.
