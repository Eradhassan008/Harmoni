# Harmoni

**Two conditions. One plan.** Harmoni helps women managing PCOS and hypothyroidism turn their care plans into one daily routine, track their progress, and walk into every doctor's visit with a clear summary.

Built at **Hackachusetts 2026** (Healthtech/Biotech track), spec-first with **Kiro**.

> **Demo only.** All patient data in this repo is synthetic. Harmoni is not a medical device, does not diagnose, and never changes or suggests medication. Always follow your care team's guidance.

---

## Why we built this

I have been living with PCOS and hypothyroidism for most of my life. Managing both means juggling two treatment plans, medications taken at different times of day, separate lab appointments, and symptoms that often overlap in confusing ways. Between doctor visits it is hard to keep track of everything, and in a short appointment it is even harder to show the full picture of what has been happening.

Harmoni is the tool I wished I had. It turns both care plans into one daily routine, lets me log how I feel in my own words, and gives my doctors a clear summary of my progress, without ever guessing at my medications.

## The problem

- PCOS and thyroid conditions are long-term and often occur together.
- Patients leave appointments with instructions from more than one clinician and see them only every few months.
- Day-to-day management (medications, habits, follow-ups, noticing what's changing) falls on the patient, with little support between visits.

## What Harmoni does

| Page | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Care Plan** | Add a plan three ways: photo upload, pasted text, or manual entry. Every item keeps the doctor's **original wording**, is tagged PCOS or Thyroid, and goes into an editable review table. Unclear items (e.g. an unreadable dose) are flagged **Needs review** and block confirmation. After the patient confirms, Harmoni builds today's checklist: Morning / Evening / Weekly / Upcoming appointments. |
| **Daily Journal** | Check off today's tasks and write a free-text note. Symptoms, mood, and period start are extracted for review before saving. The original note is always kept. |
| **Insights** | Weekly plan adherence, fatigue and acne trends, and cycle lengths, plus a plain-language **My Health Summary** built only from recorded numbers. No health or "recovery" score. |
| **Doctor Summary** | 30 / 90-day report with **Your records** separated from **Observations**, notable patterns (biggest adherence drop, biggest fatigue rise), and suggested questions for the doctor. Download as **PDF** or **CSV**. |

### Demo story: Aisha (fictional), PCOS + hypothyroidism

150 days of synthetic history show:
- Plan adherence rising from **53% to 78%** (first 30 days vs last 30 days)
- Cycle lengths becoming more regular: **57 → 45 → 39 days**
- Fatigue and acne trending down, with a stress week in mid-September detected automatically

## Safety and honesty by design

- **Never guesses medication details.** Doses and frequencies are copied exactly as written; anything unclear is marked *Needs review* and must be fixed by the patient before it is saved.
- **Patient confirms everything.** Nothing extracted is saved until the patient reviews and confirms it.
- **Every output says where it came from.** Labels distinguish *your records*, *detected automatically (rule-based, not AI)*, *AI-generated (live model)*, and *sample output (not generated by live AI)*.
- **Manual entry always works,** with or without AI.
- **No diagnoses, treatment advice, weight targets, or recovery claims.**
- **Synthetic data only.** API keys live in `.env` and are never committed. JSON backup is labeled "Demo storage - not secure medical storage."

## Run it locally

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Eradhassan008/Harmoni.git
cd Harmoni
pip install -r requirements.txt
streamlit run app.py
```

**AI status:** `ai.py` defines one adapter (`call_ai`) and reads settings from `.env` (copy `.env.example` to `.env`), but a live AI provider is **not yet connected** in this hackathon build. Harmoni runs fully with the basic text reader, manual entry, rule-based pattern detection, and clearly labeled sample outputs. Connecting a provider only requires implementing the client inside `call_ai`.

**Try it:** on Care Plan, use *Paste text* with the sample prescription, or the *Load PCOS / Thyroid sample plan* buttons.

## Known limitations

- Demo data is synthetic; records live in the browser session (with JSON download/upload to restore).
- No live AI provider is connected yet: pasted plans are read by a basic rule-based text reader, and AI sections show labeled sample output. Photo reading needs a vision-capable model to be connected.
- Pattern detection and doctor questions are rule-based (and labeled that way).
- Insights charts currently track fatigue and acne; other symptoms appear in notes and the doctor summary.
- Not HIPAA-compliant and not a medical device.
