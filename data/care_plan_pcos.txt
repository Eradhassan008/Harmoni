SAMPLE CARE PLAN (SYNTHETIC - FOR DEMO ONLY)
Endocrinology / Women's Health Clinic
Patient: Aisha K. (fictional)   Visit date: 05/25/2026
Diagnosis: Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)
Clinician: Dr. R. Lee (fictional)

Assessment:
Patient presents with irregular cycles (40-60 days), acne along jawline, fatigue,
and difficulty sleeping. Labs and ultrasound consistent with PCOS. Discussed
long-term management plan; patient engaged and has questions about cycle tracking.

Plan:
1. Medication: Continue PCOS medication as prescribed, once daily with dinner.
   Do not stop without discussing with clinic.
2. Activity: Aim for a 30-minute walk or other movement you enjoy, 5 days per week.
3. Sleep: Aim for 7-8 hours of sleep per night; keep a consistent bedtime.
4. Cycle tracking: Record the first day of each period. Note any cycle longer
   than 60 days and bring it up at follow-up.
5. Symptom log: Track acne, fatigue, mood, and hair changes weekly.
6. Stress: Try 10 minutes of a relaxing activity (stretching, breathing, journaling)
   on stressful days.

Follow-up:
- Return visit in 3 months (late August 2026).
- Blood work as ordered, 1-2 weeks before the return visit.
- Dermatology referral for acne if no improvement by next visit.

Call the clinic if: no period for more than 90 days, severe pelvic pain,
or new symptoms that concern you.
