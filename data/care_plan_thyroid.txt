Primary Care Clinic
Patient: Aisha K. (fictional)   Visit date: 06/02/2026
Diagnosis: Hypothyroidism
Clinician: Dr. M. Santos (fictional)

Assessment:
Thyroid blood test shows underactive thyroid. Patient reports tiredness,
feeling cold, dry skin, and low mood. Starting thyroid hormone replacement.
Patient also managed by endocrinology for PCOS; plans coordinated.

Plan:
1. Medication: Take thyroid medication as prescribed every morning on an empty
   stomach, 30-60 minutes before breakfast or coffee.
2. Keep thyroid medication separate from supplements such as iron or calcium,
   as discussed at the visit.
3. Take it at about the same time each day. If a dose is missed, follow the
   instructions on your prescription label or call the pharmacy.
4. Symptom check: Note energy level, feeling cold, and mood a few times a week.

Follow-up:
- Repeat thyroid blood test in 6-8 weeks (mid-to-late July 2026), then
  as directed.
- Follow-up visit after blood test results to review and adjust if needed.
- Annual thyroid check once stable.

Call the clinic if: racing heartbeat, chest pain, shakiness, or symptoms
getting worse.
